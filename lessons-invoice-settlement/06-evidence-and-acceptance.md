# Lesson 06 — Build Evidence and Capture Acceptance

Evidence should demonstrate the fact a line asserts. A polished screenshot is weak support for an unapproved scope change; an approval email is weak support that a file was actually delivered. Often both are needed.

## Evidence categories

| Claim | Useful evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Work was requested | signed scope, purchase order, ticket, approved change |
| Work occurred | time record, meeting note, activity log |
| Deliverable exists | final document, repository release, published page, screenshot |
| Deliverable was sent | delivery email, portal submission, transmittal log |
| Customer accepted | sign-off, approved milestone, ticket closure, acceptance email |
| Amount is correct | rate card, calculation schedule, expense receipt, tax calculation |

Name links for what they prove: “Approved homepage screenshot” is better than “Drive link.” The workbook's paired asset-name and asset-URL columns follow this pattern, and LedgerLane supports named proof links on each invoice line.

## Evidence quality

Good evidence is relevant, attributable, dated, readable, and durable. Verify that the intended reviewer can open the link without requesting access. Prefer a stable final artifact over an editable working file. If a live URL may change, preserve an approved snapshot according to privacy and retention rules.

Do not link secrets, credentials, unnecessary personal information, or customer-confidential material to broadly shared reports. Use least-privilege access and redact unrelated data.

## Acceptance is a decision

Define what constitutes acceptance in the agreement. It might be explicit sign-off, passage of a review window without a valid rejection, completion of a test, or approval by a named role. A task moved to “done” internally is not automatically customer acceptance.

Record:

- what version was reviewed;
- acceptance criteria;
- approver and authority;
- decision date;
- conditions or exceptions; and
- link to the decision.

## Evidence gaps

Never replace missing evidence with a misleading label. Mark the gap, assign an owner, and decide whether to hold the line, use alternative corroboration, or disclose the limitation. The workbook includes “TODO: Get meeting notes” examples; these are useful preparation flags but should be resolved before claiming the notes as proof.

## Final evidence check

Open every link from a reviewer-equivalent account. Confirm the label matches the destination, the version is final, the billing period is consistent, and the artifact supports the specific line rather than merely the overall project.
