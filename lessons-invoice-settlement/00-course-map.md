# Invoice Settlement Fundamentals: Course Map

Invoice settlement is the controlled path from an agreement and evidence of delivery to an accepted balance, payment, and closed record. It is broader than merely sending an invoice. A sound process answers five questions:

1. What was promised?
2. What was delivered, and when?
3. How was the amount determined?
4. Who accepted the obligation and authorized payment?
5. Can another person reconstruct the transaction later?

This course uses the same working model as LedgerLane: projects contain work items; invoice lines may have sub-lines; each line may carry a client-facing title, billing period, hours, evidence links, and an amount; and an engagement can be one-time, recurring, barter, or a community partnership. The example workbook adds useful patterns such as stable line keys, parent/child deliverables, monthly hour columns, and up to three named evidence links.

## How to use the sample CSV files

Each lesson includes a small, editable CSV worksheet populated with fictional data. Download a sample to rehearse the lesson’s reconciliation or control, then replace the example values with appropriately protected working data. URLs under `example.com` are placeholders; they do not contain evidence. The samples are teaching aids rather than accounting-system or LedgerLane import templates.

## How to use the lessons

Read in order the first time. Then use Lessons 03 and 12 as operating references. The examples teach process, not jurisdiction-specific tax or accounting advice. Invoice numbering, tax content, retention periods, electronic-invoicing rules, and revenue recognition vary by contract and jurisdiction; confirm them with the responsible finance or legal professional.
