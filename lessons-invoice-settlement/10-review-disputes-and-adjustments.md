# Lesson 10 — Review, Disputes, and Adjustments

## Use a two-stage review

The preparer confirms source data and assembles the schedule. A reviewer checks contractual eligibility, arithmetic, evidence, customer requirements, and payment details. For higher-risk settlements, the approver should not be the same person who entered the work or can change bank details.

Record reviewer, date, scope, exceptions, and resolution. Approval should apply to a frozen version, not an ever-changing live view.

## Triage customer questions

Classify each exception:

- **information request** — customer needs clarification or evidence;
- **scope dispute** — customer says work was not authorized;
- **performance dispute** — output allegedly failed acceptance criteria;
- **quantity/rate dispute** — hours, units, rate, tax, or arithmetic differs;
- **administrative rejection** — missing PO, vendor data, format, or portal step;
- **payment exception** — approved but late, partial, short, or misapplied.

Assign an owner and target date. Keep undisputed lines moving if the agreement and customer process allow.

## Preserve history

Do not overwrite an issued invoice or delete an inconvenient line to make the record appear clean. Preserve:

1. the original issued document;
2. the customer's question or rejection;
3. supporting analysis and approvals;
4. the credit note, debit note, or revised invoice; and
5. the final resolution.

Use unique revision identifiers and reference the original. A corrected invoice should not share an ambiguous identity with the prior version.

## Adjustments

An adjustment needs a reason and authority. Examples include contractual discount, service credit, duplicate reversal, tax correction, write-off, or agreed compromise. Distinguish a commercial credit from a cash receipt. Never backdate an approval or conceal an error by editing source hours.

## Root-cause review

After resolution, ask why the exception passed preparation. Frequent causes include unclear scope, stale rate cards, weak cut-off, inaccessible links, duplicate parent/child totals, missing PO data, and unclear acceptance. Fix the upstream control rather than merely adding more attachments to the next invoice.
