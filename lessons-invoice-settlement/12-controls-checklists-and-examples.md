# Lesson 12 — Controls, Checklists, and Worked Examples

## Pre-settlement checklist

- [ ] Correct legal customer, project, engagement, and billing contact
- [ ] Contract/PO and approved changes identified
- [ ] Billing basis, currency, rates, caps, tax, and terms confirmed
- [ ] Cut-off and billing period applied consistently
- [ ] Every included line is contractually billable
- [ ] Client-facing titles are understandable
- [ ] Parent/sub-line presentation does not double count
- [ ] Hours, rates, fees, credits, and total independently recalculated
- [ ] Evidence links are named, accessible, final, and relevant
- [ ] Acceptance is present where required
- [ ] Engagement total and task amounts do not overlap
- [ ] Required invoice fields and submission attachments are complete
- [ ] Preparer/reviewer approval is recorded against the issued version

## Worked example A: supported time-based settlement

Agreement: September consulting at $150/hour, capped at 20 hours, with evidence by deliverable.

| Deliverable | Hours | Evidence | Calculation |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| Discovery interviews | 6 | Interview notes and customer acknowledgment | $900 |
| Process map | 8 | Approved process-map PDF | $1,200 |
| Implementation workshop | 7 | Agenda, attendance, and outcomes | $1,050 |

Worked hours are 21, but the cap permits 20 billable hours. The schedule should not silently change the source log. Show the 21-hour reconciliation, a 1-hour non-billable cap adjustment, and a $3,000 subtotal. If the customer pre-approved an overage, link that approval and apply the agreed rule instead.

## Worked example B: fixed-fee parent with detail

Agreement: $8,000 when the content system is accepted.

- **Content system — $8,000**
  - Keyword universe — 10 internal hours
  - Article briefing templates — 12 internal hours
  - Publishing roadmap — 8 internal hours

The child hours support delivery and margin review; they do not multiply the fixed fee. Link the accepted final artifacts to the relevant sub-lines and the formal acceptance to the parent.

## Worked example C: mixed barter and cash

Agreement: consulting is exchanged for venue access plus a $500 travel reimbursement. Record the venue access as non-cash consideration with its delivery evidence and the $500 as the cash component. Do not label the whole arrangement cash-settled merely because one component is cash. Confirm any valuation and tax treatment separately.

## Post-settlement checklist

- [ ] Issued document and delivery proof retained
- [ ] Customer approval or exception logged
- [ ] Receipt/remittance or reciprocal performance matched
- [ ] Partial payments, fees, withholding, and credits resolved
- [ ] Zero balance has a documented explanation
- [ ] Revision history preserved
- [ ] Sensitive records access-controlled
- [ ] Recurring errors assigned for process improvement

## Final test

Give the close packet to a knowledgeable person who did not prepare it. If they can reproduce the scope, quantity, price, approvals, transfer, and final balance without oral explanation, the settlement record is doing its job.
